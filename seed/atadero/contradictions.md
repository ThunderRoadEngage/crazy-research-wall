# Jaryd Atadero: where the sources disagree

Each item lists the conflicting accounts with evidence ids from `evidence.csv` and their URLs. Labels: **[news]**, **[official]**, **[research]** (Mystic's own Thunder Street write-up, "TRE"), **[forum]** (CanAm/Paulides, low reliability). These are disagreements to resolve. None of them is a finding.

## C01. Distance of the clothing site from the trail
- About 150 ft from where he was last seen, about 1.25 mi up the trail: Sheriff Alderden, EV022 [news] https://www.9news.com/article/news/missing-boy-likely-attacked-by-animal/73-345346437
- About 550 ft from the trail: EV034 [news/AP] https://www.myplainview.com/news/article/Hikers-in-Co-Find-Clothes-of-Missing-Boy-8943975.php
- 436 vertical ft and 1,000 ft off the trail, about 1.25 mi up: EV035 [news] https://www.greeleytribune.com/2003/06/11/trying-to-cope/
- 500 vertical ft and 1,050 lateral ft, near Camp 2 (SAR report): EV008 [news] COL19
- 550 vertical ft with 1,500 ft of ground distance: TRE case page [research], the source logged under EV042. "500+ ft, over two miles from the trailhead": EV041 [forum] https://www.canammissing.com/jaryd-atadero.html
- Last-seen point at 1.5 mi from the trailhead: EV037 [official] https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/PLAW-106publ324/html/PLAW-106publ324.htm and EV003 COL19. The 2003 accounts above say 1.25 mi.

## C02. When the remains were found after the clothing
- June 14, 2003, 11 days later: EV010 and EV011 COL19, EV018 https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_of_Jaryd_Atadero
- "The next day, searchers found Jaryd's skull nearby": EV020 [news, search excerpt] https://www.coloradoan.com/story/news/2025/04/17/father-of-jaryd-atadero-dies-26-years-after-sons-disappearance/83126936007/
- "About a week later" (TRE timeline) against "ten days after the initial discovery" (TRE2): EV042 TRE, EV054 TRE2 [research]

## C03. Day the clothing was found
- June 4, 2003 (a Wednesday): EV008 COL19, EV018 Wikipedia, EV054 TRE2
- "Thursday": EV034 (AP, June 9) and EV033 (CBS/AP, June 11, "last Thursday") https://www.cbsnews.com/news/shredded-clothes-tell-sad-story-10-06-2003/

## C04. Cause and manner of death
- Death certificate reads "undetermined, probable mountain lion attack": EV012 COL19, EV020 COL25
- Wikipedia says the certificate ruled death "probably caused by a mountain lion" and also says the cause "has never been officially classified": EV018
- "There was never an official cause of death given": EV023 [news] https://www.9news.com/article/news/community/20-years-after-he-disappeared-what-happened-to-jaryd-atadero/73-9e7b06e2-617a-409a-b1cc-a1c6e17c467a
- The NecroSearch report, as quoted through Allyn's book in TRE1, calls a large carnivore "most plausible" but says the clothing was removed before significant bleeding or decomposition: EV053 [research]. Specialists emailed by Allyn in 2015 doubted a lion attack: EV052 [research].

## C05. Which theory the Sheriff's Office held
- Predator: Alderden in 2003, EV022 and EV035. Smith and Nelson, EV013 COL19 and EV021 https://www.denver7.com/news/local-news/25-years-later-how-3-year-old-jaryd-ataderos-disappearance-changed-missing-children-cases-in-colorado
- Fall or exposure: "investigators believe he probably died of a fall... or of exposure," EV029 [news] https://www.latimes.com/archives/la-xpm-1999-oct-09-mn-20607-story.html
- Drowning: Allyn in 2000 said "The sheriff thinks he drowned," EV025 https://extras.denverpost.com/news/news1003j.htm. TRE and TRE1 say the sheriff pushed the lion theory publicly but privately said drowning: EV045, EV051 [research]. CanAm says the same, EV041 [forum].
- TRE1 quotes Supplemental Report 99-06-59.S02, p. 6: dive teams reported a 95–100% probability of detection. That figure argues against drowning: EV051.

## C06. The 1999 tracks
- Allyn, Oct 1999: "where the cougar prints and a little person's prints come together, the child's prints disappear": EV032 https://www.cbsnews.com/news/no-service-for-missing-boy/
- Sgt. Smith: the cat tracks were about 0.1 mi from the child prints, with no sign the paths intersected: EV024 https://extras.denverpost.com/news/news1007.htm
- DOW and trackers: the "child" prints were possibly or actually bear tracks. The lion prints were confirmed, and a boot print was still thought to be Jaryd's: EV024, EV028 https://www.latimes.com/archives/la-xpm-1999-oct-07-mn-19754-story.html
- TRE1, citing the LCSO supplemental report p. 5: the lion tracks were older than the small prints, and tracker "Seaux" was certain the cat never saw Jaryd: EV051 [research]. TRE1 also says the tracks "intersected." That conflicts with Smith's 1999 statement.

## C07. Distances: Fort Collins to the trail, and resort to the trail
- Fort Collins to the trail, by source: 20 mi (EV028, AP), 43 mi NW (TRE1), about 48.1 mi (TRE), 50 mi (EV026 CNN https://www.cnn.com/US/9910/05/colorado.missing.boy.01/), 60 mi (EV001 COL19). USFS driving directions come to about 11 + 49 = 60 road miles: EV040 https://www.fs.usda.gov/r02/arp/recreation/big-south-trailhead
- Resort to the trail: 15 mi (EV002 COL19), about 16.5 mi (TRE), 20 mi (EV041 CanAm). The resort was "45 minutes west of Fort Collins" (TRE). That figure doesn't fit a resort 15 mi short of a trail 60 mi out.

## C08. Hike location given as "near Twin Lakes"
- AP/LA Times, Oct 5, 1999: "on a hike ... near Twin Lakes": EV027 https://www.latimes.com/archives/la-xpm-1999-oct-05-mn-19115-story.html. All other sources say the Big South Trail. This is probably an early reporting error. It remains unresolved.

## C09. Size of the search
- About 70 searchers: EV029 ("as many as 70"), EV031 ("nearly 70") https://www.deseret.com/1999/10/7/19469360/hope-dwindles-for-3-year-old-lost-in-colorado-mountains/, EV028 (69 on Wednesday)
- More than 200: EV007 COL19 (SAR report timeline) and EV035 Greeley 2003 ("more than 200 volunteers, law officials and search-rescue dogs"). The difference may be the daily count against the cumulative count.

## C10. Length of the search
- Six days: EV025 (Denver Post 2000), EV033 (CBS/AP 2003)
- A week, suspended Friday Oct 8: EV007 COL19, EV029
- Seven days: EV038 (Congressional Record) https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CRECB-2000-pt11/html/CRECB-2000-pt11-Pg16127.htm
- "Weeks of searching ensued": EV022 (9News 2003)
- "Little more than a week": TRE timeline, EV042
- The CBS story dated Oct 7, 1999 (a Thursday) reports the search called off "on Friday." The page date looks wrong: EV032.

## C11. The helicopter crash: injuries, day, and cause
- Five aboard, five injured, none with life-threatening injuries: EV026 CNN, EV027 LAT. "Four people... injured": EV032 CBS. Two had significant injuries: EV006 COL19.
- Sheets's leg gash: 13-inch (EV016 COL19) against 11-inch (EV036 https://www.greeleytribune.com/2007/11/18/rock-solid-loveland-man-continues-to-anchor-larimer-county-search-and-rescue-despite-a-near-fatal-helicopter-crash/)
- Greeley 2007 calls it "Saturday afternoon on Oct. 3, 1999." Oct 3 was a Sunday, and the SAR timeline has the crash on Sunday, Oct 3 at 3:30 p.m.: EV006.
- Cause: the Air Force investigation found the aircraft too low and too slow, and a downdraft sank it (EV036). Sheets blames too much fuel (EV036 and EV006). TRE says it was "flying too low over the river" (EV045). The SAR timeline places it on the "upper Big South Trail."

## C12. Whether the recovery area was searched in 1999
- The SAR report says foot searchers never reached 9,120 ft, and the helicopter "would have likely searched the area had it not crashed": EV012 COL19
- Greeley 2003: "Officials did search the area by helicopter for two days before the helicopter crashed": EV035
- TRE: the sheriff refused to check Seaux's ridge, saying a helicopter "had already been up there": EV046 [research]

## C13. Whether the finders had been in the area before
- Greeley 2003: "The two men said they had not been in the area before": EV035
- Osborne in 2019: "we were up there a week earlier and talked quite a bit about the various scenarios": EV008 COL19

## C14. Spelling of a finder's first name
- "Gary Watts": EV008 COL19, EV035, EV018 Wikipedia
- "Gareth Watts": EV054 TRE2 [research]

## C15. Group structure and how well members knew each other
- 11 members plus the two children: EV001 COL19, EV024, EV029. "11 or 12 people": EV026 CNN.
- Two groups: EV026 CNN, EV038 Congressional Record, EV029. Three loose groups: EV042 TRE, citing the Sheriff's report.
- "Friends of his father's" and "many of whom are friends": EV025, EV024. TRE says "no one had been introduced to one another and there was no organization to the group" and calls it "familiar and unfamiliar individuals": EV042.

## C16. Time of day he was last seen
- The SAR report puts the fishermen's sighting at 11:30 a.m.: EV003. TRE says the group lost sight of him about 11:20, then the scream came between 11:30 and noon: EV042.
- Public Law 106-324: "last seen the morning of October 2": EV037. AP: "disappeared Saturday afternoon": EV030 https://www.deseret.com/1999/10/6/19469204/2-fishermen-report-seeing-missing-boy-in-mountains/
- The outing's start: left the resort at 10 a.m. (SAR, EV003) against a decision at 9:00 and arrival at the trailhead at 10:30 (TRE, EV042).

## C17. Where the group went first
- The group told Allyn they would only go to the hatchery, then decided to hike: EV001 COL19. TRE says the group went to the hatchery, was dissatisfied, made a brief stop at Poudre Falls without telling Allyn, and then went to the trail: EV042. No news source mentions Poudre Falls.

## C18. DNA identification
- Initial results: 86% (EV011 COL19, EV042 TRE), 87% (EV054 TRE2), 85% (EV041 CanAm)
- Final 100% confirmation: "a decade later" (EV011 COL19), "16 years after" by Technical Associates (EV054 TRE2), "years later... No results paperwork was provided" (EV050 TRE). The 2025 Coloradoan gives no timing.
- Tooth: TRE and CanAm report that an outside expert saw a mixture of two DNA profiles and that the tooth was consumed in testing (EV050, EV041). No official source addresses this.

## C19. Hair analysis
- The two hair types would be analyzed (EV022, EV035). CBI tests "revealed little" (EV012).
- The hairs were non-human and not mountain lion, and the report was not given to the family: EV041 [forum]. TRE1 and TRE2 say they were not from a mountain lion "or any other predatory animal": EV051, EV054 [research].

## C20. Condition of the jacket
- "Appeared to be puncture marks": EV008 COL19. "Multiple puncture holes": EV035.
- "In the photos, the jacket appeared intact": EV033 CBS/AP
- TRE2: "showed no signs of an attack." Osborne tried to show Allyn claw holes near the right shoulder: EV054 [research]. The TRE photos (media.csv, jacket-1 to jacket-8) show holes and tears marked with orange cards.

## C21. Clothing worn and items not recovered
- Last seen wearing a hooded sweatshirt, sweatpants, and a fleece jacket: EV026 CNN. Recovered: shoes, the fleece jacket or pullover, and the sweatpants (EV008, EV033).
- Not recovered: underwear and a blue tank top (EV054 TRE2). The lower jaw, teeth, T-shirt, and underwear (EV041 CanAm). No source accounts for the hooded sweatshirt CNN describes.

## C22. Length of the trail
- 11 miles: EV002 COL19. 10.4 miles one-way: EV040 USFS.

## C23. Family and volunteer access to the trail
- TRE and CanAm say the family was threatened with arrest if they went on the trail or aided the search, and that the family entered posing as birdwatchers: EV045 [research], EV041 [forum]
- COL19 says Nelson took Allyn and his family on a hike up the trail to talk things out: EV013 (same article)
- After the search was suspended, COL19 says "others were allowed to continue the search on their own." AP quotes Deputy Gordon reversing course: searchers "cannot go back": EV029.

## C24. Mesa Verde sighting and the Fort Collins woman
- Denver Post 2000: sighting reports at Mesa Verde and elsewhere "nothing has panned out": EV025
- COL19: a Fort Collins woman was charged for falsely claiming she knew the abductor: EV014
- TRE: the Mesa Verde lead was "completely ignored by authorities, despite claims made by the Sheriff that they called Mesa Verde officials." The volunteer who brought it forward was later arrested for fabricating information: EV047 [research]. Whether she is the same woman COL19 describes is unconfirmed.

## C25. Arlyn Atadero's location in 1999

- "Came out from California to support his brother" and "Arlyn was in California": EV041 [forum]
